## Short intro (use above or beside the diagram)

Most consultants pick a lane: strategy or execution. Change Maven works both, at the same time.

The Dual-Pyramid Model is how we structure every engagement. One pyramid handles strategy, the other handles execution, and both are mapped to the same diagnostic spine: CEO/Vision → Departments → Management → Frontline Workers. Wherever your organization needs help, there's a matching service on both sides.

## Strategy Pyramid

- **Executive Interventions** — Executive coaching and strategic plan updates for leadership.
- **Organizational Alignment** — Org-wide transformation initiatives and department-level workshops & seminars.
- **Targeted Education** — Leadership development, Six Sigma (Green & Black Belt), change management concepts, and continuous process improvement.

## Execution Pyramid

- **Technical Automation** — AI automation, process mapping, project management, and IV&V for system implementations.
- **Change Management Integration** — Stakeholder analyses, system audits, and pay-scale audits.
- **Methodological Standards** — Built on ACMP, PMI, and ASQ certified methodology.

## Closing line (pull quote / tagline)

"We match your organization's needs to globally recognized standards, driving real people and culture adoption at every level."
