001 / Core Competencies

What we deliver

Project and Program Management

  • Implementation project management for system, technology, and process rollouts, from kickoff through go-live
  • Schedule, risk, and stakeholder management, with clear status reporting to leadership and sponsors
  • PMO setup and embedded PM support to a government or prime program manager

Change Management and User Adoption

  • Change readiness and impact assessments, stakeholder analysis, and communications planning
  • User adoption and resistance management for new systems and processes
  • Leadership alignment and sponsor coaching so change is led from the top

Training and Workforce Development

  • Lean Six Sigma training and certification (Yellow, Green, and Black Belt) as an authorized GLSS reseller
  • Workshops in project management, leadership, and continuous improvement, in person or virtual
  • Curriculum and instructional design, including facilitated cohorts built around real client projects

Also offered. Emergency preparedness tabletop exercises and continuity planning, delivered with a safety training partner when the scope calls for it.

002 / Differentiators

What sets us apart

Veteran leadership

Founded by a U.S. Army veteran who started as an engineer. Mission focus, discipline, and fluency with how federal and military programs actually run.

Capability, not dependency

We teach the method while we deliver the work. Your people come out trained and practiced, so the next project doesn't need a consultant.
