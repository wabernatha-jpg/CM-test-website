01We Define the Destination

Strategic Planning

You know where you are, and where you're going.

Before anything gets built, trained on, or rolled out, someone has to know exactly where the organization stands, and where it's actually headed. Every engagement starts here: an honest read on the current state, and a clear, executive-level picture of what comes next.

Current-State Assessment & Stakeholder Interviews

A grounded, first-hand read on where things actually stand today, gathered directly from the people doing the work, not just the org chart.

Organizational Assessment

A structured look at how the organization is actually built to operate today, so the plan accounts for real constraints instead of assumed ones.

Executive & Board-Level Vision Setting

Working sessions with leadership to define what "better" actually looks like, in specific enough terms that the rest of the engagement has something to aim at.

Leadership Initiative Review

Schedule a Consultation

Strategic planning helps your leadership team guide high-impact initiatives through structured direction, review, and support. Through facilitated leadership reviews and alignment sessions, leaders keep initiatives aligned with strategy, supported, and on track to deliver measurable results.

Schedule a Consultation

Why This Matters

Even well-chosen initiatives underperform when leadership direction is inconsistent or unclear. Organizations commonly run into the same four problems:

  • Initiatives drift away from strategic intent.
  • Leaders have limited visibility into progress and risks.
  • Unresolved barriers slow down execution.
  • There is a weak connection between leadership and results.

Strengthening leadership direction and review discipline is the fastest way to improve initiative outcomes.

The Leadership Engagement Model

Direction

Keeping every initiative aligned with strategic objectives.

Progress Review

Structured leadership checkpoints that assess outcomes and trajectory.

Obstacle Removal

Identifying and clearing the barriers that stand in the way of success.

The Outcome

Stronger leadership direction, clearer visibility into progress, and faster removal of obstacles, so initiatives deliver measurable results and leadership's impact shows across the organization.

Engagement Scope

24 hours in total. The schedule is flexible.

  • Launch and conclusion: 2 hours each
  • Ten review meetings: 2 hours each, 20 hours total
  1. 1

    2 hours · Start here

    Strategic Alignment Session Launch

    Preparation and a kickoff meeting that set direction for the initiatives leadership will be guiding.

  2. 2

    10 meetings · 2 hours each, 20 hours total

    Leadership Initiative Review Meetings

    Ten structured reviews, each with preparation, follow-up, and the meeting itself, that check progress and clear obstacles as initiatives move forward.

  3. 3

    2 hours · Wrap up

    Strategic Alignment Session Conclusion

    Preparation and a closing meeting that confirm alignment and the impact the initiatives have delivered. This cadence builds leadership guidance directly into execution.

02We Build the Team There

Building Capability

You have the ability to get there.

A strategy is only as good as the people who have to carry it out. Once the destination is set, we build the ability directly into your team, through certified training, hands-on coaching, and skill-building sessions designed to stick long after we've left the room.

Leadership Development & Executive Coaching

One-on-one coaching for the leaders who have to model the change themselves before they can ask anyone else to.

Six Sigma Training & Certification

Certified methodology your team can apply immediately, not just a credential to file away.

Skill-Building Workshops

Focused sessions built around your team's specific gap, with a workbook and action-plan guide so it doesn't evaporate after the session ends.

03We Create the Tools Needed

Building Capacity

You have the platform to cross it.

Capability without a platform to run on stalls out. We build the operational capacity: the processes, systems, and hands-on sessions that turn a plan and a trained team into something that actually runs day to day.

Kaizen Events & Tabletop Exercises

Focused, hands-on sessions that surface real process problems and work through them with the people who own the work.

Workshop Facilitation

Structured facilitation that keeps cross-functional groups moving toward a decision, not stuck circling the same conversation.

Operational Efficiency Implementation

Getting process and system improvements installed and running, not just documented in a report.

04We Make the Change Stick

Sustaining the Change

You keep driving forward.

The real test of a change isn't the rollout: it's what's still standing a year later. We stay engaged past the finish line to keep it alive: aligning the organization around it, planning for who carries it forward, and keeping the team engaged in it long after the initial push fades.

Change Management Support

Ongoing support that keeps the change from quietly reverting once the project team moves on.

Organizational Alignment

Keeping teams, incentives, and priorities pointed at the same outcome, so the change doesn't get quietly undone by conflicting goals.

Succession Planning & Team Engagement

Making sure the change doesn't depend on any one person staying in their seat, and that the team stays invested in it.

PMP CCMP Six Sigma Black Belt

Built on certified methodology. Every engagement runs on these standards: the same discipline behind all four phases of bridging the gap.
