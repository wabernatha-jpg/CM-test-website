Change Management Integration

Stakeholder analyses, system audits, and pay-scale audits.

Change Management Integration is the middle layer of the Execution Pyramid — where the technical work gets checked against the people it affects. We audit the systems and the impact on stakeholders side by side, so nothing ships without a plan for who it changes and how.

Stakeholder Analyses

Mapping who's affected by a change, how much, and what they need to hear before it happens — not after.

System Audits

An independent look at whether a system or process is doing what it's supposed to, before it becomes someone else's problem.

Pay-Scale Audits

Reviewing compensation structures for consistency and fairness as roles and responsibilities shift during a reorganization.
