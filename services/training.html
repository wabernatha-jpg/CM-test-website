Training & Facilitation

Workshops, tabletop exercises, seminars, retreats, Kaizen and team-building events, certified training, and coaching. Every session ends with something you can use: a process map, an action plan, a workbook.

Whether your team needs a skill, a decision, or a plan, we run the session that gets you there. Standardized where the methodology is proven, custom where your situation isn't.

Facilitated Events

When the whole team needs to be in the room. We design and run the session so it ends in a decision, a plan, or a shared way of working, not just a good conversation.

Workshops

Focused sessions built around your team's specific gap, using your organization's actual tools and terminology. Participants leave with a workbook and an action-plan guide, so it doesn't evaporate after the session ends.

Tabletop Exercises

Scenario-driven sessions that pressure-test a plan before it's live, so the gaps show up in a conference room, not during the actual rollout.

Seminars

Teaching-led sessions that give a group a shared vocabulary and a practical method for one topic, such as change management, project management, or systems. Everyone leaves working from the same playbook.

Retreats

Off-site time for a leadership team to step away from the day-to-day and work on direction, priorities, and how the team works together. We facilitate so the retreat ends in decisions and an action plan, not just a good conversation.

Training & Coaching

Change sticks when the people doing the work, and the leaders modeling it, have the skills to do it differently, not just the instruction to.

Six Sigma Certification

Certification earned on a real problem, not a simulated one. Delivered through our Go Lean Six Sigma partnership, with coaching plans for individuals and for teams.

Kaizen Events

Focused, short-cycle improvement sessions aimed at one process or one problem, built to surface root cause and commit to a fix in the same room.

Team-Building Events

Facilitated sessions that help a team understand how each person works and how to work better together. Built around the real friction your team is dealing with, not generic icebreakers.

Executive & Leadership Coaching

Active inquiry coaching, one-on-one or in a small group, meeting biweekly over six-month contracts. A performance-improvement-plan variant is available.

See Executive & Leadership Coaching

An online assessment, a 32-page career-focused report, and a one-hour webinar on what your results mean for how you communicate and lead. Included in many of our one-on-one engagements.
