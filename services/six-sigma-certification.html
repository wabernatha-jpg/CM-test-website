How 1-on-1 Coaching Works

You're paired with a Master Black Belt for six hours of live web meetings or calls. Instead of working through the curriculum alone, you have an experienced practitioner in the room while you apply it to a project that matters to your organization.

The six hours are split into one hour for project selection, then a one-hour tollgate review after each DMAIC phase. Each review is a checkpoint: your Master Black Belt looks at the work you've done, and you move on when it's ready.

To get the most from the time, schedule each coaching meeting after you finish the matching training module.

Live Coaching

How Group Coaching Works

Group Coaching is built for teams who want to complete their certification project together instead of on their own. Three one-hour live sessions create the structure and the dialogue between your learners and their Master Black Belt, focused on moving high-value projects forward, not just reviewing material.

Sessions are scheduled 4 to 5 weeks apart. After the third session, your team has 4 to 6 weeks to finish their projects before the Master Black Belt reviews and approves the work for certification.

Between live sessions, coaching doesn't stop. Every group gets unlimited access to their Master Black Belt through a discussion board built for the group, so learners can connect with each other and get an answer without waiting for the next call.

Group Size

Up to 15 learners, working across up to 3 projects, all coached by the same Master Black Belt from kickoff to certification.

Ongoing Support

A dedicated group discussion board keeps coaching available between sessions, not just during them.

This engagement focuses on leadership direction and review. For the full picture of how we assess and plan, see Strategic Planning.

Why This Matters

Even well-chosen initiatives underperform when leadership direction is inconsistent or unclear. Organizations commonly run into the same four problems:

  • Initiatives drift away from strategic intent.
  • Leaders have limited visibility into progress and risks.
  • Unresolved barriers slow down execution.
  • There is a weak connection between leadership and results.

Strengthening leadership direction and review discipline is the fastest way to improve initiative outcomes.

The Leadership Engagement Model

Direction

Keeping every initiative aligned with strategic objectives.

Progress Review

Structured leadership checkpoints that assess outcomes and trajectory.

Obstacle Removal

Identifying and clearing the barriers that stand in the way of success.

The Outcome

Stronger leadership direction, clearer visibility into progress, and faster removal of obstacles, so initiatives deliver measurable results and leadership's impact shows across the organization.

Engagement Scope

24 hours in total. The schedule is flexible.

  1. 1

    2 hours · Start here

    Strategic Alignment Session Launch

    Preparation and a kickoff meeting that set direction for the initiatives leadership will be guiding.

  2. 2

    10 meetings · 2 hours each, 20 hours total

    Leadership Initiative Review Meetings

    Ten structured reviews, each with preparation, follow-up, and the meeting itself, that check progress and clear obstacles as initiatives move forward.

  3. 3

    2 hours · Wrap up

    Strategic Alignment Session Conclusion

    Preparation and a closing meeting that confirm alignment and the impact the initiatives have delivered. This cadence builds leadership guidance directly into execution.
