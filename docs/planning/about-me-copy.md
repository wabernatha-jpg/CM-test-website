# About Me — Website Copy

For the About Me / About Change Maven section of the website, paired with a photo of Wesley.

---

My path here started in the U.S. Army, where I learned something that's stuck with me ever since: everyone has a piece of the pie. Every person in an organization owns a piece of what happens next, and change only works when they know it.

From there I spent two decades in corporate America running continuous improvement projects across the globe. The higher I climbed, the clearer it became: change isn't about process, it's about people. Culture, stakeholder buy-in, and the human side of transformation matter more than any framework on a slide.

I built on that with an executive master's in international business from Saint Louis University, an MBA, and certification as an executive coach, plus PMP, ACMP, and ASQ credentials to keep the discipline sharp. But the real education came from watching good initiatives succeed or fail based on whether people felt like partners in the change or just targets of it.

Now I'm bringing that foundation home. I founded Change Maven to help nonprofits and local organizations here in the Alton area navigate change with intentionality, clarity, and people at the center, because transformation works best when everyone understands their role in it.
