Diagnostic Partner

Change Maven + Predixxa

Predixxa tells us, in hard numbers, where an organization is misfiring. Change Maven does the work of fixing it — coaching the people, redesigning the process, and making the change stick. Neither half works as well alone.

Most change engagements start with a hunch. Ours starts with a diagnostic. Predixxa is an organizational-alignment analytics platform — a data layer we bring in ahead of the work, so the plan is built on what's actually happening inside the organization, not just what leadership assumes is happening.

Alignment Diagnostic

Analytics that surface misalignment across six operating dimensions — before it shows up as missed targets or stalled initiatives.

Certified OAS Coaching

A certified Organizational Alignment Specialist program that develops managers, not just informs them.

Pulse4™ Monitoring

Ongoing tracking of four key performance indicators, so progress is measured after the engagement ends, not just at kickoff.
